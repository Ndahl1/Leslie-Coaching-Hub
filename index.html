Run the Wheel

1 · Open warm"Mr. and Mrs. ___, this is Nels, the sales manager at Leslie. I never let a consultant leave without checking in personally — did we take good care of you today?"
2 · Acknowledge · Empathize · By-Pass"I can absolutely understand that. If I were in your shoes I'd feel the same way. Let's suppose you did make a decision today — which option would you choose?"
Isolate the logical"My job is to inform you so you can make the best decision for your situation. If you don't mind me asking — what questions are you still thinking about?"
50 / 40 / 10"Half is materials and labor. About 40% is what it takes to be in business — the trucks, the training, the people who answer at 2 AM. The last 10% is what we keep if everything goes perfect. Can you see how our pricing is transparent and fair? Should we go ahead?"
Inverted wheel"Every project is Time, People, Company, and System. The System never changes — same equipment. So the only way to cut the price is to shrink the circle. What distinguishing factor lets them charge less for this project?"
Next Availability Install Program"When we have a gap in the install schedule, filling it earns you a discount. What would the number have to be?" (No number → step down $200–300 at a time.)
5-year math — go slow, write it down"Over five years: the repairs you'll keep making, plus what the old system overpays in energy, plus the replacement you'll end up buying anyway — against replacing today under full warranty. Can you see how it actually saves you to replace today?"
Like / dislike → suppose"What did you like about option B? What do you think [spouse] would say? Suppose [spouse] is on board — which option?" Then: call them now → swing back tonight → book the second appointment.
Home inspector story"Any system over 10–12 years gets flagged in the inspection, and buyers negotiate ten, twelve thousand off. You're buying the system either way — the only question is whether you get to use it."
4 · The ask — a question, not a statement"Should we go ahead and provide this service for you then?"
Still no — protect & date it"Completely understand — you two talk it over. I'm going to have [consultant] check back with you Thursday evening — does that work? And the quote is locked until then."
